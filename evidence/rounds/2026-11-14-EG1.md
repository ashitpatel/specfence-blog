# EG1: borrowed from the Elephant-Goldfish Model (2026-11-14)

Owner (chat, 2026-11-14): "let's definitely add the gaps identified, with clear attribution to EGM. Also, add
capability to work upstream and downstream with EGM. Ask users first and then consume/generate artifacts accordingly."
Decision record: `docs/project/decisions/2026-11-14-owner-decisions-eg1.md`. Source comparison:
`docs/project/reviews/2026-11-14-elephant-goldfish-model.md`.

**Attribution.** The Elephant and Goldfish roles, the zero-context reader as a test of a document, the rubric
"comprehension check", and the synthesizer that gathers reviewers' findings are from Google's Elephant-Goldfish Model
(https://github.com/google/elephant-goldfish-model, Apache-2.0; authors of its architecture doc Jim Quinn, Elise Ewing
and Dave Rensin), after Dave Rensin's essay "Elephants, Goldfish and the New Golden Age of Software Engineering" (2026,
https://research.google/pubs/elephants-goldfish-and-the-new-golden-age-of-software-engineering/). No EGM code or text is
copied; the ideas are cited in the module docstrings, the skills, the tool results (`origin`), the export file and
plan 5.4. What specfence adds is the referee: a reading is judged by Alloy where it can be.

| **GF2** | the comprehension check | a fresh reader restates each requirement and marks the unclear ones; each becomes an `unclear` question | `record_reading` (kind restate) |

## Evidence

- Tests: `tests/test_readings.py` (18), `tests/test_readings_tools.py` (4), `tests/test_integrations.py` (+9 EGM).
  The prototype ambiguity: "the approver of an expense is not its owner" read as sets that differ (the agent's model)
  and as no shared person (both readers); Alloy shows the situation where the owner is one of several approvers. The
  two readers agree with each other.
- **Mutants: 13 of 13 killed** (each mechanism broken on purpose, the three test files run): no context in the
  per-requirement runs, no canary check, no currency check, no derived declaration constraints, gate ignoring unasked
  findings, no carry check, gate always off, default multiplicity lost, no ask-first, no drift record, export copying
  sentences, `done_check` unwired, no grouping. The context mutant survived at first; the test that kills it
  (`test_only_the_requirement_that_differs_in_context_is_reported`) was written for it.
- `tests/test_rules.py` passes: every new refusal has a negative test (Rule 2).

## Limits (stated, not fixed)

- **Freshness is not proven.** The canary catches a reader forked from the drafting conversation that obeys the brief;
  it does not catch host instruction files injected into a fresh context (EGM issue #237 reports this on some hosts).
- **Shared declarations are not compared.** A field with an arrow, `disj`, `var` or `this`, and `abstract`, stay as
  the model wrote them in the readers' vocabulary (`shared_declarations` lists them). Step 3b still asks those choices.
  A sig with an appended fact is refused: move it into a named fact.
- **Untagged declarations localise poorly.** A multiplicity in an untagged sig shows under "declarations"; the finding
  says to tag the sig with its `@req`.
- **Restatements are compared by the agent** that wrote the document (no check). Story reads are advisory, not gated.
- **`forge_done`'s wiring has no test of its own** (the same `done_reasons` call is tested through `done_check`).
- **Cost.** Two formalize readers and one restater per final round; an answer that edits the document needs another
  round before done. Not measured.
- **Not measured on seeded defects.** Plan 5.3's seeded requirement defects are the bench: does the fresh-reader arm
  find seeded ambiguities the checker-only arm misses, at what false-alarm rate and token cost? Owed; the owner
  deferred a pre-registration ("Not now", 2026-11-14).
- EGM itself was not installed or run here; the interop is by its documented file conventions (README, architecture
  doc, `docs/harnesses.md`, read 2026-11-14).
