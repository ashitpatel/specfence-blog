## ST4.5: scale verdict (session-measured, approximate)

- Triage: ~7 agent-minutes per read (21 reads). Gate models: ~30–40 minutes
  per model (write + debug + confirm + hand-read). End to end ≈ 45–90
  agent-minutes per confirmed structural defect (triage amortized).
- Depth-2 STANDS: at 0.20 fenced precision, deeper budgets spend ~1 agent-hour
  per extra candidate for mostly-noise. The lever is generation precision
  (fewer, better candidates), not depth. Revisit after a precision round
  with more than 2 positives.
- The track's economics vs temporal: temporal is mechanical (skeletons +
  solver); structural is agent-heavy per item (models + reading). ST1's
  confirmation ladder is what makes the agent time settle into verdicts
  instead of advisories.
