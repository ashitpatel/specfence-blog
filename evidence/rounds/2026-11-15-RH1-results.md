## What this says

- **A rehearsal is far more careful than a trial.** With the skill as almost its only reading, and nothing else to
  do, a fresh agent follows the outside-input paragraph, v1's included. In the trials, the same paragraph was read
  and passed over during hours of engine output and questions to the person.
- **The failure is about load, not text.** That is the limit stated in advance ("no person and no long session").
  It means the FC1 v1 failure came from the work around the instruction, not from the wording.
- **No cheap text-only check reproduced it.** EGM's review (EG2), the lint (SL1) and the rehearsal (RH1) all failed
  to show it.
- **What did change behaviour** in both FC1 fixes was a gate that holds `done`. That is the case for EH1, the
  escape-hatch audit (pre-registered in 49ea24c), over any further work on checking the text.
- **The rehearsal still has one use.** It shows what a careful agent makes of the text. Here that includes tagging
  U3, which no trial reached. That speaks to whether the text can be followed, not to whether it will be.

**Nothing is changed in the skills or the engine.**
