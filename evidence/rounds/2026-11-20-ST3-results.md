(T5, T11 — stop rule satisfied), precision 2/10 = 0.20 (target ≥ 0.20),
eligible noise cut 53% (target ≥ 50%). Fires: F-b ×3, F-c ×21, F-d twins

## ST3.1–3.3: extraction — correct, low-yield; coverage prediction missed

Mechanism: `skeleton/extract.py` (decidable shapes incl. membership tests
and ignored logging), wired into `generate()` with param-bound steps and
guard preds, ST3.3 soundness via independent recheck (mismatch demotes).
11 tests (incl. an Alloy-solve e2e), 3/3 mutants (`mutate_st3.py`).

Coverage (honest miss): ~0.03 whole-tree (klippy 7/258, aiopika 3/100,
apscheduler 0/5), 0.00 on the six triage classes (0/71), vs ≥ 0.40
predicted. Cause: real methods call helpers, loop, await, or do I/O —
fence-correct rejections, all of them; attrs/dataclass state is invisible
to `_fields_and_ops` (MemoryDataStore has no skeleton at all). No unsound
widening was attempted to chase the number. Consequence for ST4: hand
model-writing stays the bottleneck; extraction pays only for simple
synchronous container classes.
