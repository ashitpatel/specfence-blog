## ST3.0: generation-precision fences (no tuning without the oracle)

Mechanism: `_candidate` (`index/structure.py`) marks fenced shapes; the
structural scorer applies ×0.25 (below the 1.5 threshold for typical scores);
the why-line names the fence. Three fences:

- **F-a merged-only:** a group whose fields were never ALL written by one
  method (union-find merged pairs, never jointly written) — T1's position
  triple is the shape (the why-text "one method writes together" is false
  for merged groups; the text is fixed to say "linked" for merged-only).
- **F-b single-writer-no-partial:** a group with one writer and empty partial
  (T2, T10, T12-as-indexed, T13, T14) — consistent by construction, nothing
  to diverge.
- **F-c property-less doc/shape:** invariant-doc or parent-link with no
  co-write group and no checkable property (T15, T17, T18, T19, T20, T21).
  T16 (co-write + doc) is explicitly kept.

M5's shape (joint write broken ⇒ pair vanishes) is investigated, not
preregistered to ship: a disjoint-partials rule ships only if it keeps both
reals eligible without cratering precision on the 21.
