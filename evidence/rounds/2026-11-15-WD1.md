# WD1: a confirmed bug leaves the report only with the person's yes

- **The hatch:** CM1 rows F4 and F5 (`2026-11-15-CM1-results.md`). A statically confirmed bug left section 1 in
  either of two ways, and the report never said so:
  - withdrawn with any non-empty reason;
  - superseded by a model-level finding, which needs no evidence.
- **The owner's decision:** "Person's yes + list (Recommended)" (`decisions/2026-11-15-owner-decisions-wd1.md`).

## What changed

**What is protected** (`repro.protected`):
- a bug, or a deterministic defect;
- any finding whose evidence is "statically confirmed" or stronger.

**The log** (`repro.FindingLog`):
- **`withdraw`** refuses a protected finding unless `decided_by` is the person's accepting elicitation record.
- **`report(..., supersedes=)`** refuses to supersede a protected finding with a lower status (a bug with a
  model-level finding) or weaker evidence. Superseding with evidence at least as strong still works: that is a
  corrected bug, which stays in section 1.
- **Proposals the person has not agreed to** go to `withdrawal-proposals.jsonl`. They are kept out of
  `findings.jsonl`, because `evals/cleanroom/score.py` counts every row there that is not a withdrawal as a finding.

**The tool** (`withdraw_finding`, now async with `ctx`). For a protected finding it asks the person by elicitation,
with a yes/no question that quotes the finding and the agent's reason:

| what happens | result |
|---|---|
| the person says yes | it is withdrawn, with their elicitation record |
| the person says no | it is kept, the proposal is recorded as "kept by the person", and the question is answered |
| the person declines | it is kept, and the question stays open |
| the person cannot be asked (no forms, the shell, pinned driver mode) | it is kept, and the question stays open |

An unprotected finding is withdrawn as before. The question kind is `withdraw-finding` in `elicitations.py`, so
`open_questions` and `approvals_open` list it.

**The report** (`survey/report.first_look`):
- an entry in section 1 or 2 with a pending proposal carries `withdrawal_proposed` (its reason, its outcome and why);
- `withdrawn_findings` lists every withdrawn or superseded finding, with its reason, `superseded_by`, and
  `withdrawn_by` (the person or the agent).

**The skill.** model-slice's withdrawal paragraph states the rule, marked **checked**: WD1. It tells the agent to
say why it thinks the finding no longer stands, and never to re-report it weaker to get round the question.

- **Two failures were intended.** `test_defects_cleanroom_paramiko::test_withdraw_and_supersede_a_finding` and
  `test_r109_list_without_java::test_withdraw_and_open_questions_run_without_java` called `withdraw_finding`

**A regression found after the suite, and fixed.** The shell CLI refuses every tool that takes `ctx`. So making
`withdraw_finding` async stopped shell users withdrawing even a model-level finding. The CLI now runs the tools in
`cli.ASKS_ONLY_SOMETIMES` (only `withdraw_finding`) with no client. That withdraws an unprotected finding, and keeps a
protected one with its question recorded open. A test covers both cases (11 WD1 tests in all). The related CLI,
rules, CM1 and WD1 tests pass, 82 in all.

## Limits

- **In pinned driver mode, a protected finding cannot be withdrawn at all.** The envelope has no section for
  withdrawals, so the question stays open. That fails safe.
- **An agent can still report a second, weaker finding without superseding the bug.** The bug then stays in
  section 1, so nothing is hidden.
- **A person's yes is only as good as their reading.** The question quotes the finding and the reason, as the gate
  audit's last point says.
- **No agent trial was run.**
